ABOUT LUNARMATCH

LUNARMATCH is a lunar image correspondence platform designed to assist in comparing surface imagery through feature detection, matching and geometric verification.

The platform combines image preprocessing, visual feature extraction, descriptor matching and robust geometric analysis to estimate the correspondence between two lunar images.
